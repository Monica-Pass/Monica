Source comparison also used v1.4.0, 48ebac7d088c79f61fcacbfb2c31f56bc101a9c0.

Changes: separate default-off consent switches, framework-managed boolean-only preferences,
root/system-only binder delivery, Android 14-16 and primary-user Xiaomi-family gates,
validated GMS destinations, exact class/method lookup, no ART memory offsets or Unsafe,
no Android 17 beta adaptation, no hot reload, no vault/data access. Failure to identify a
method leaves that behavior unchanged. Physical HyperOS devices have not been tested.

Diagnostic design also references dingwen07/CredentialProviderManager v0.1.1,
c7eb00934327b54fb39753d7bacd14e4a955e1f3, GPL-3.0:
https://github.com/dingwen07/CredentialProviderManager
No testOnly flag or direct hidden-settings access was copied.
Existing configuration backend remains adapted from cr-zhichen/password-manager-switch (MIT).
